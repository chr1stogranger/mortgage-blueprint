# Mortgage Blueprint Borrower Guide

**Mortgage Blueprint — powered by RealStack**

Your loan officer built you a Blueprint: a live, private view of your home loan options. You can change the numbers, compare options and see exactly what you'd pay. Your loan officer sees your changes, so you can work out the plan together.

Blueprint is an estimate tool. Your official Loan Estimate and Closing Disclosure come from your loan officer.

## 1. Open your Blueprint

1. **Tap the link** your loan officer sent you by email or text.
2. You'll see **"Sign in to view your Blueprint."** Pick one:
   - **Continue with Google**: the fastest option if your email is a Gmail or Google account.
   - **Sign in with email**: type your email and tap **Send magic link**.
3. If you chose email, check your inbox for **"Your RealStack Blueprint sign-in code."** Type the code into **Code from email** and tap **Verify code**.
   - The code works once and expires in 60 minutes.
   - The email has no sign-in link on purpose. Just type the code.
4. You'll see **"Welcome back, {your name}"**, and your Blueprint opens.

> **Use the same email your loan officer has for you.** The link only opens for the people on your file. That's what keeps your numbers private.

You only sign in once per device. Next time, the link opens straight to your Blueprint.

**Keep a copy.** You may see **"Keep a copy of this blueprint?"** at the bottom. Tap **Add to my Blueprints** to save it to your own account. Then it's on every device you sign in on, even without the link.

## 2. If something goes wrong signing in

| You see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **This blueprint is private** | You signed in with an email that isn't on your file | Tap **Use a different email** and use the one your loan officer has. Or ask them to add the new email. |
| **Almost ready** | Your loan officer is still building your numbers | Tap **Check again** later, or text your loan officer. |
| **Link Not Found** | The link is old or was mistyped | Use the **Email**, **Call** or **Schedule** buttons on that screen to reach your loan officer for a fresh link. |
| No code email | It's in spam, or it's been over an hour | Check spam and promotions for "Your RealStack Blueprint sign-in code," or use **Continue with Google**. |

## 3. Get it on your phone

- **iPhone (app):** search **Mortgage Blueprint** in the App Store.
- **iPhone (no download):** open your link in **Safari**, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open the link in **Chrome**, tap the menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

It opens full-screen like any other app.

## 4. Find your way around

**On a phone**, the bottom bar has **Overview · Compare · PricePoint · Share · More**. **More** holds the rest, including **Get Pre-Approved**.

**On a computer**, everything is in the left sidebar.

| Section | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Your main page. Payment, costs, cash to close and qualification, top to bottom. Use **On this page** to jump to a section. |
| **Compare** | All your options side by side. |
| **Workspace** (computer only) | Two or three calculators at once. Good for "sell this home, buy that one" or "refi vs. keep." |
| **Learn** | Plain-English explainers. |
| **Team** | Your deal team (loan officer, agent, escrow), with tap-to-call, plus who has access. |
| **Share** | Email, copy, save as PDF, and Get Pre-Approved. |
| **Settings** | Light/dark mode, privacy options, PIN lock. |

Depending on your loan, your loan officer may also turn on sections like **Refi Summary**, **Seller Net** (what you'd walk away with from a sale), **Rent vs Buy**, **Investor** or **Prop 19**.

## 5. Change the numbers

Tap any number to change it. Everything recalculates instantly.

**The most useful things to try:**
- **Purchase price.** What happens at $50,000 more or less?
- **Down payment.** Compare 5%, 10% and 20% (watch the mortgage insurance change).
- **Rate and points.** The rate ladder shows whether paying points makes sense for how long you'll keep the loan.
- **Property tax, insurance and HOA.** Use the real numbers from a listing you like.

**What you'll see:**
- **Monthly Payment:** principal, interest, taxes, insurance, HOA and mortgage insurance.
- **Cash to Close:** down payment + closing costs + prepaids − credits.
- **Pre-Qualified?** Whether the payment fits your income and debts.
- **True cost of owning:** your full payment, minus tax savings, principal you're paying down and appreciation.

**Some numbers are locked.** If a scenario says **"View only. Your loan officer has locked this scenario,"** it's a fixed quote. Make a copy (scenario menu → **Duplicate**) or ask your loan officer if you want to play with it. Some income figures are entered by your loan officer from your documents.

**Made a mistake?** Changes save automatically. Open the scenario menu → **Version history**, and pick **Restore this version** or **Open as a copy**. A version is kept every 10 minutes while you're editing.

## 6. Compare your options

- Your loan officer may have built a few options for you (for example, "FHA 3.5% down" vs. "Conventional 10% down").
- Open **Compare** to see them side by side.
- To try your own idea, open the scenario menu and tap **Duplicate**, then change the copy. Your loan officer will see it.

## 7. Share, save and send

From the **Share** tab:
- **Email**: opens **Email Me This Worksheet**. Tap **Send to my email** to get a PDF copy in your inbox. Your loan officer is copied.
- **Save Scenario as PDF**: download the worksheet (or the Refi Summary for a refinance).
- **Copy Link**: copy your Blueprint link. Only people on your access list can open it (see the next section).

## 8. Add your spouse, co-borrower or agent

1. Go to **Team**, then the **Sharing** card (**People with access**).
2. Type their email and tap **Add**.
3. Send them your link. They sign in with **that** email.

You can remove anyone you added with ×. People your loan officer added stay on unless your loan officer removes them.

## 9. Working together with your loan officer

- **Your changes save automatically**, and your loan officer can see them.
- **On a call together?** You'll both see a **Live** bar with each other's name. Your loan officer can follow along as you move through the screens, and you'll see an outline around the field the other person is in.
- **Questions?** **Team** has your loan officer's phone and email. Tap to call.

## 10. Ready to move forward

When the numbers look right, tap **Get Pre-Approved Now** on the Share tab (or **More → Get Pre-Approved** on a phone). Your loan officer gets your request with the scenario you were looking at, and will reach out with next steps and a secure application link.

After you close, your loan officer may set up a **My Home** tab. There you can open your home report: your home's value, your equity, and when a refinance might make sense.

## 11. Your account and privacy

- **Who can see your Blueprint:** you, the people on your access list, and your loan officer's team. Nobody else, even with the link.
- **You must be 18 or older** to use Blueprint. See the Terms (`blueprint.realstack.app/terms`) and Privacy Policy (`blueprint.realstack.app/privacy`).
- **My Account.** Open `blueprint.realstack.app` directly (not through your share link) and tap your name or **Sign in** at the top. From there you can:
  - turn **Cloud sync** on or off
  - **Export my data (JSON)**
  - **Sign out**
  - **Delete my account** (type DELETE to confirm). This removes your cloud data and sign-in. Copies already saved on your device stay there.
- **Settings → Security & Privacy:**
  - **Privacy Mode** hides dollar amounts on screen.
  - **PIN Lock** asks for a PIN to open the app on this device.
- **Settings → Appearance:**
  - **Theme** switches between light and dark.
  - **Animated background** turns the moving background on or off.
- **Changing your email isn't supported yet.** Ask your loan officer to add your new email to your file, then sign in with it.

**Using Blueprint on your own (no link).** The calculator at `blueprint.realstack.app` is free and needs no account. Your work stays on that device. Tap **Sign in** → **Save your Blueprint** to back it up and sync it to your other devices. You'll check a box confirming you're 18+ and agreeing to the Terms.

## 12. Extras: PricePoint and My Home

- **PricePoint** (the middle button on a phone) is a daily home-price guessing game. Guess what real homes sold for, or predict what a listing will sell for. Tap **Blueprint** on any home to run its payment. No sign-up is needed. Sign in to save your streak across devices.
  - Every PricePoint email has a one-click unsubscribe.
- **Markets** is a play-money prediction game. Balances have no cash value, and you must be 18 or older.

## 13. Quick answers

**Is this my official loan quote?**
No. Blueprint gives estimates. Your official numbers come on your Loan Estimate from your loan officer.

**Does changing numbers here change my loan?**
No. It only changes your Blueprint. Your loan officer sees what you tried and can talk it through with you.

**Do I need a password?**
No. Use Google or the emailed code.

**Can my realtor see it?**
Only if you or your loan officer add their email under People with access.

**I'm on a new phone.**
Open your link and sign in again with the same email.

**Who do I contact?**
Your loan officer. Their name, phone and email are on the **Team** tab and on every page you share.
