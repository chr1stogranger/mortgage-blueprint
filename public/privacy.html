1. Introduction

Mortgage Blueprint ("the App") is a mortgage calculator and educational tool developed by Chris Granger, a licensed mortgage broker (NMLS #952015) operating under Xpert Home Lending, Inc. (NMLS #2179191). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information across our web application (blueprint.realstack.app) and mobile applications (iOS and Android).

2. Three Ways to Use the App — and What Each Stores

a. Without an account (the default)

Loan scenario inputs, property details, and financial profile details (purchase price, down payment, interest rate, income, debts, FICO score, assets) are processed on your device and saved in your browser's local storage. In this mode, this data is not transmitted to or stored on our servers, and it is deleted when you clear your browser data or uninstall the App.

b. With an optional account and cloud sync

If you create an account (via email sign-in link or Google) and turn on cloud sync, the blueprints you choose to upload are stored in our database (hosted by Supabase) so they stay backed up and sync across your devices. You choose what uploads — nothing is uploaded silently. You can turn sync off, export everything as a file, or permanently delete your account and all cloud data at any time from Account settings in the App.

c. Blueprints shared with you by a loan officer

If your loan officer shares a pre-filled blueprint with you via a secure link, that blueprint (and the edits you both make) is stored in our database so you can collaborate in real time. Access requires signing in, and access controls limit each blueprint to you and your loan officer.

4. How Your Data Is Protected

  • All connections use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit.
  • Cloud data is stored with Supabase (SOC 2 Type II audited) and encrypted at rest.
  • Database row-level security restricts every record to its owner (you) and, for shared blueprints, your loan officer.
  • As a licensed mortgage professional, we treat financial data you provide in connection with loan services as nonpublic personal information under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act and safeguard it accordingly.

5. Third-Party Services

The App connects to the following external services. These connections transmit only the minimum information needed to fulfill the request:

Supabase (Database & Authentication)

Stores account and cloud-synced blueprint data and processes sign-ins. Privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy.

Google Sign-In (optional)

If you choose "Continue with Google," Google shares your name, email address, and profile photo with us to create your account. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Gmail Access (optional, loan-officer accounts only)

Loan officers on our team may optionally connect their Gmail account so the App can send loan summary emails from their own address and read mortgage-related messages they choose to view inside the App. If granted, the App requests the Google scopes gmail.send and gmail.readonly. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: Gmail data is used only to provide these user-facing features, is never used for advertising, and is never sold or transferred to third parties. Gmail access tokens are stored only on your device and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Borrower accounts are never asked for Gmail access.

Resend (Email Delivery)

Delivers sign-in links and notification emails to the address you provide. Privacy policy: resend.com/legal/privacy-policy.

FRED API (Federal Reserve Economic Data)

Used to display current mortgage interest rates. No personal information is included in this request. FRED's privacy policy is available at fred.stlouisfed.org/legal.

RapidAPI (Real Estate Data)

Used by the PricePoint feature to display real estate listings for the price-guessing game. The App sends zip code queries to retrieve public listing data. No personal information is included. RapidAPI's privacy policy is available at rapidapi.com/privacy.

Vercel (Hosting)

The App is hosted on Vercel. Vercel may collect standard server logs including IP addresses, browser type, and access timestamps as part of normal web hosting operations. Vercel's privacy policy is available at vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

6. Cookies and Tracking

The App does not use advertising trackers, ad pixels, or cross-site tracking scripts. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar services. Local storage on your device is used for your calculator data, your sign-in session, and (for loan officers who connect Gmail) a short-lived Gmail access token.

7. Data Sharing and Sales

We do not sell, rent, lease, or share your personal information with any third party for their own use. Period.

The App does not participate in data broker networks, lead generation services, or advertising networks. Your blueprints are visible only to you and — for blueprints created or shared by your loan officer — that loan officer.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local data: stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the App.
  • Account & cloud blueprints: kept until you delete them or delete your account (Account → Delete my account, available in-app on all platforms). Deletion is permanent and removes your cloud blueprints, edit history, and sign-in.
  • Loan officer records: if you work with a loan officer, records they maintain for your loan file (contact details, shared blueprint records) are business records they are required to retain under applicable law and are not deleted by deleting your App account.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (including under the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to know, access, correct, export, and delete the personal information we hold about you, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. The App provides self-service export (Account → Export my data) and deletion (Account → Delete my account). For anything else, contact us using the information below — we respond within 30 days.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is a financial calculator intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will address the concern promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to how we handle personal information, we will provide notice within the App. The July 2026 update describes the introduction of optional accounts and cloud sync.
